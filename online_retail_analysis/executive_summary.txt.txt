#Executive Summary

This project analyzes online retail transaction data from a UK-based retail company between 2009 and 2011.
The goal of the analysis is to extract business insights related to customer behavior, country performance, sales trends, and purchasing patterns.

#Key Metrics#
Total Revenue: £17,743,429.17
Total Orders: 36,975
Best-Selling Product: White Hanging Heart T-Light Holder
Top Performing Country: United Kingdom
Average Order Value (AOV): £479.88
Units Per Transaction (UPT): 289.95

#Key Insights#
The distribution of Average Order Value shows that most customers place relatively low-value orders, while a small group of customers contributes disproportionately high spending.
More than 90% of total revenue comes from the United Kingdom, indicating a strong geographic concentration of sales.
The top two customers have very similar purchase volumes, showing that a small change in customer retention or engagement strategy could significantly affect total revenue.
Monthly sales trends reveal two major sales peaks during the analyzed period, while sales remain significantly lower during most other months of the year.
The high Units Per Transaction value suggests that a large portion of purchases are made in bulk, indicating strong wholesale purchasing behavior.

#Business Impact#
The business is highly dependent on a small number of high-value customers.
Revenue concentration in a single country increases business risk and limits international growth opportunities.
Monthly sales fluctuations indicate unstable revenue patterns throughout the year.

#Recommendations#
Introduce seasonal campaigns, monthly promotions, or discount events during low-sales periods to stabilize monthly revenue.
Provide premium support, loyalty programs, or VIP services for high-value customers to improve retention.
Develop targeted engagement strategies for top customers, since small increases in their purchase volume could generate significant additional revenue.
Since bulk purchasing behavior is strong, the company could expand wholesale-focused services, pricing models, or partnership programs.
